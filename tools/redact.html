Frequently Asked Questions

Is the text actually removed, or just covered?

Unlike simple markup tools, ZapPDF flattens the document. This means the text underneath the black box is permanently destroyed and cannot be highlighted or extracted.

Can I redact images as well as text?

Yes, you can draw a redaction box over any part of the page, including images, logos, and signatures.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Redaction is a highly sensitive task, which is why doing it locally in your browser with ZapPDF is much safer than using an online server.
