Frequently Asked Questions

How do I extract specific pages?

Enter your desired pages in the range input field. For example, typing "2-5, 8" will extract pages 2, 3, 4, 5, and 8.

Does splitting affect the original file?

No, ZapPDF generates a brand new PDF file containing only your selected pages. Your original file remains untouched.
