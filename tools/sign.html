Frequently Asked Questions

Can I delete multiple pages?

Yes! Simply click the trash can icon on any page thumbnail you wish to remove before saving.

Is it safe to sign sensitive documents here?

Absolutely. Because ZapPDF is a client-side app, your sensitive files are never uploaded to the cloud.

Can I undo a deleted page?

Currently, you must reload the PDF if you accidentally delete a page. We recommend double-checking before saving.
