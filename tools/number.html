Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change where the numbers appear?

Currently, numbers are automatically stamped neatly at the bottom center of every page.

Does it work on large documents?

Yes! ZapPDF can process hundreds of pages instantly thanks to our optimized WebAssembly engine.

Will it overwrite existing text at the bottom?

The page numbers are stamped near the very edge of the page margin, but we recommend checking your document to ensure there is sufficient margin space.
