Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Zero Tracking Cookies

ZapPDF is fundamentally designed around user privacy. We do not use any tracking cookies, third-party analytics cookies, or advertising cookies. Your activity is your own business.

2. Local Storage

Instead of traditional cookies, we may use your browser's "Local Storage" API. This is exclusively used to remember your UI preferences (like Dark Mode settings or last used compression level) to make your next visit more convenient.

3. No Third-Party Access

Because we use local storage instead of cookies, the data is never sent to our servers (because we don't have any), nor is it accessible by third parties. It remains strictly on your device.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository.
